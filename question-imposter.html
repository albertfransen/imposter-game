How to Play

Game Overview

Everyone gets a question to answer. But one person gets a DIFFERENT question - they're the imposter!

The Trick

The imposter's question is designed so their answer could fit with the others. Can you spot whose answer seems... off?

How to Play

  1. Pass the device - each player sees their question privately
  2. Everyone answers their question out loud (go around the circle)
  3. Discuss - whose answer seems suspicious?
  4. Vote on who you think had the different question!

Example

Regular players see: "How many countries have you visited?"

Imposter sees: "Pick a number between 0 and 15"

Everyone says a number - but can you tell who's guessing?

Winning

  • Regular Players Win: Correctly identify the imposter
  • Imposter Wins: Avoid detection by blending in!
